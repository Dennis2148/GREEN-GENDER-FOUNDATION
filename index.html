Our Key Focus Areas

We tackle the most pressing challenges facing young people and women in our communities.

Menstrual Health

We fight period poverty by promoting menstrual health education, distributing sanitary products and supporting vulnerable girls to manage menstruation with dignity. We strive to ensure that no girl misses educational opportunities because of a lack of menstrual hygiene products.

Prevent Early Pregnancy

We promote awareness, mentorship, life skills and informed decision-making among adolescents to prevent early pregnancies. We also support teenage mothers through mentorship, skills development and opportunities to rebuild their futures.

GBV Prevention

We advocate against gender-based violence affecting both boys and girls, as well as women. We promote awareness, prevention, protection, reporting and access to appropriate support services while encouraging communities to challenge harmful practices and discrimination.

Mental Wellness

Promoting emotional resilience, safe spaces, and awareness of mental health challenges among youth and women.

Climate Action and Environmental Sustainability

We encourage women and young people to participate in environmental conservation, climate awareness and sustainable community practices, promoting climate resilience and environmental responsibility.

Women and community Empowerment

We support women and vulnerable households through capacity building, skills development, advocacy and economic empowerment initiatives aimed at improving livelihoods and strengthening community participation.

Boy Child Initiative

Empowering the Boy Child

The Boy Child Initiative is a programme being developed under Green Gender Foundation to address challenges affecting boys and adolescent young men in our communities.

  • Promoting positive masculinity and responsible manhood.
  • Providing mentorship and leadership development opportunities.
  • Creating awareness about mental health and emotional well-being.
  • Promoting responsible decision-making.
  • preventing violence, abuse and harmful social practices.
  • Promoting gender equality and healthy relationships.
  • Providing safe spaces for boys to discuss challenges affecting their lives.
  • supporting boys to identify their talents and develop their potential.

Our goal is to raise responsible, confident, respectful and empowered boys who will become positive contributors to their families and communities.

About Green Gender Foundation

Green Gender Foundation is a community driven foundation dedicated to empowering young people both boys and girls—and women to become confident, informed and active participants in their communities. We work alongside communities to address social, gender and environmental challenges that affect children, adolescents, young people and women, particularly those facing vulnerability or limited access to opportunities. Through community empowerment, mentorship, advocacy and climate action, we create spaces where people can build skills, understand their rights, access support and contribute to positive community change. We believe every child and young person deserves dignity, safety, equal opportunity and the chance to shape a better future regardless of gender or background. The foundation was established in response to challenges affecting vulnerable communities, particularly adolescent girls, teenage mothers, young boys and women. These challenges include period poverty, early and teenage pregnancies, gender-based violence (GBV), limited mentorship opportunities, social inequalities and environmental challenges. The foundation believes that empowering young people and women through education, mentorship, advocacy and community engagement is essential in building stronger, healthier and more resilient communities. Its work is guided by the understanding that both boys and girls deserve equal opportunities, protection, dignity and a chance to contribute positively to society. The Founder is Everline Museo muema who is still the director

Our Story & Mission

Green Gender Foundation was founded to create a world where every young person regardless of gender can thrive with dignity. We address root causes of inequality through education, mentorship, and community-led programs.

Our approach is holistic: we work with schools, local leaders, and families to ensure sustainable change. From providing sanitary products to championing climate action, we believe in empowering communities to build their own solutions.
